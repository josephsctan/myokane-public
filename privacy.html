MyOkane — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · App: MyOkane for Android (nz.effortless.myokane) · Publisher: Effortless, New Zealand

MyOkane is a personal and family app. It keeps your receipts in a Google Sheet that you create, or that someone shares with you, and it includes a few games (Minesweeper, a points-only dice game and a two-team scoreboard). The app has no server of its own: it talks directly to Google services using your Google account. This policy explains what data the app handles and where that data goes.

What the app handles

  • Your Google account email and name. The app uses these to sign you in. Your email is stored only on your phone, so you don't need to sign in each time. We never receive it.
  • Your Google Sheet, through Google Drive file access. The app asks for the Google drive.file permission, which only lets it use spreadsheets that it created for you or that you choose yourself in Google's file picker. It cannot see your other Drive files. You can either let the app create a new sheet in your Drive or pick an existing one. The app also asks Google whether you own the chosen sheet (file metadata only), to decide whether to show owner settings.
  • Receipts. These are the merchant, date, amount, category and notes you enter or scan. They're read from and written to your chosen Google Sheet through the Google Sheets API, using your own Google sign-in. A copy is kept on your phone, so the app works offline.
  • Other tabs in your sheet. The sheet can also hold an Access tab (the owner lists the email addresses of people who may use each part of the app), a Settings tab and a Leaderboard tab. These live in the sheet, which the owner controls.
  • Receipt photos (only when you scan). Receipt scanning is not yet available to the general public: for now it is limited to a small set of approved accounts, and we plan to support it for all users in the future. When a user scans a receipt with the camera, or picks a photo, the image is sent to Google's Gemini model (via Google Firebase AI Logic). This is only to read the merchant, date, total and category from it. The app doesn't store the photo. Google processes it under its own terms, and under those terms Google may keep and use the content to improve its services. Please don't scan documents with information you don't want to share with Google.
  • Game scores. If you get a top-10 time, you can choose to add a name to the leaderboard. Only the name you type, your score and the date are written to the sheet's Leaderboard tab. Everyone the sheet is shared with can see them. Your email is never added. Scoreboard team names and history stay on your phone and the history is not kept after the app closes.
  • App integrity checks. The app uses Google Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity, to confirm that requests come from the genuine app on a real device. Google handles this under its own terms.
  • Settings on your phone. These include your mute choice, the name you last used, your game progress, your last open page and your cached receipts. They stay on the device.
  • Demo mode. "Try the demo" on the sign-in screen opens the app with made-up sample data. It needs no account, makes no network requests, and stores nothing: everything is discarded when you exit the demo.

What the app doesn't do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no selling or renting of data.
  • No data is sent to any server run by Effortless. There isn't one.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or other personal data is collected. The camera is used only when you choose to scan a receipt.
  • The app has no payments, and the dice game uses points only; there is no real money.

Google user data

MyOkane's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses Google user data only to provide its features (sign-in, creating or reading your chosen sheet, and syncing your receipts). It does not use it for advertising, does not sell it, and does not allow people to read it except as needed to provide the features you ask for (for example, other people the sheet is shared with can see the sheet's contents).

Sharing

Your receipts, the Access list and leaderboard entries are visible to anyone the spreadsheet is shared with. The sheet's owner controls that sharing in Google Sheets. The Access tab controls what the app shows each person; it is not a security boundary, so please share the sheet only with people you trust. Data goes to Google as described above, for Sign-In, Sheets and Drive file access, Gemini via Firebase, and App Check/Play Integrity. It goes to no one else.

Security

All traffic to Google uses HTTPS. The app accesses a spreadsheet only with your own Google sign-in and permission, and you can revoke that at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • The app has no accounts of its own: you sign in with Google. There is nothing to delete at Effortless because Effortless holds no data.
  • Receipts and leaderboard rows live in your Google Sheet until someone with edit access deletes them. To remove all of your data, delete the sheet (or the rows) in Google Sheets.
  • Sign out in the app, or uninstall it, to remove everything it has stored on your phone.
  • To remove the app's access to your Google account, revoke it at the link above.
  • To ask for help deleting your data, email us (see below).

Children

MyOkane isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
