MyOkane — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026 · App: MyOkane for Android (nz.effortless.myokane) · Publisher: Effortless, New Zealand

MyOkane is a personal receipts app. It keeps your receipts in a Google Sheet that you (or the sheet's owner) control, and it includes a small Minesweeper game. The app has no server of its own: it talks directly to Google services using your Google account. This policy explains what data the app handles and where that data goes.

What the app handles

  • Your Google account email and name. The app uses these to sign you in, and to check that the MyOkane spreadsheet has been shared with you. Your email is stored only on your phone, so you don't need to sign in each time. We never receive it.
  • Receipts. These are the merchant, date, amount, category and notes you enter or scan. They're read from and written to the MyOkane Google Sheet through the Google Sheets API, using your own Google sign-in. A copy is kept on your phone, so the app works offline.
  • Receipt photos (only when you scan). When you scan a receipt with the camera, or pick a photo, the image is sent to Google's Gemini model (via Google Firebase AI Logic). This is only to read the merchant, date, total and category from it. The app doesn't store the photo. Google processes it under its own terms, and under those terms Google may keep and use the content to improve its services. Please don't scan documents with information you don't want to share with Google.
  • Game scores. If you get a top-10 time, you can choose to add a name to the leaderboard. Only the name you type, your score and the date are written to the spreadsheet's Leaderboard tab. Everyone the sheet is shared with can see them. Your email is never added.
  • App integrity checks. The app uses Google Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity, to confirm that requests come from the genuine app on a real device. Google handles this under its own terms.
  • Settings on your phone. These include your mute choice, the name you last used, your game progress and your cached receipts. They stay on the device.

What the app doesn't do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no selling or renting of data.
  • No data is sent to any server run by Effortless. There isn't one.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or other personal data is collected. The camera is used only when you choose to scan a receipt.

Sharing

Your receipts and leaderboard entries are visible to anyone the spreadsheet is shared with. The sheet's owner controls that sharing in Google Sheets. Data goes to Google as described above, for Sign-In, Sheets, Gemini via Firebase, and App Check/Play Integrity. It goes to no one else.

Security

All traffic to Google uses HTTPS. The app accesses the spreadsheet only with your own Google sign-in and permission, and you can revoke that at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Receipts and leaderboard rows live in the Google Sheet until someone with edit access deletes them.
  • Sign out in the app, or uninstall it, to remove everything it has stored on your phone.
  • To remove the app's access to your Google account, revoke it at the link above.
  • To ask for help deleting your data, email us (see below).

Children

MyOkane isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
